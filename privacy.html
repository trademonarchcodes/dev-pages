DEVPAGES · PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information DEVPAGES collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you when you create an account, manage a developer profile or publish a website. It applies to the DEVPAGES platform and dashboard, not to the independent content or privacy practices of websites hosted by users.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers and account-related security information.
  • Developer profile information: preferred developer name, biography, optional portfolio URL, profile picture or logo, and your choice about displaying a developer credit.
  • Hosting and website information: requested website names, assigned website addresses, uploaded website files, deployment information, hosting status and related technical records.
  • Payment information: transaction references, amounts, currency, payment status and related billing records. Payments are processed by the payment provider. DEVPAGES does not ask you to send card details or passwords to support.
  • Support and communications: information you choose to provide when contacting us about your account or service.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated when you use the platform, such as IP address, device/browser details, timestamps, security events and service logs, where made available by our systems or providers.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; display and manage public developer profiles; provide website-address allocation, uploads, publishing and hosting; process and reconcile payments; provide support; prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents; maintain and improve the platform; comply with legal obligations; and enforce our terms.

3. Public information and published websites

Your public developer profile may show your developer name, bio, profile picture or logo and portfolio link. If you enable developer credit, your developer name may appear as a clickable credit on websites you publish, linking to your portfolio or DEVPAGES profile. Published website files are intended to be publicly accessible at their website addresses. Do not upload private or confidential information into a public website or public profile.

4. Service providers and sharing

We share only the information reasonably needed with providers that help operate DEVPAGES, including:

  • Supabase: account authentication, database and developer-profile image storage.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, deployment, delivery and related infrastructure.
  • Paystack: payment checkout, transaction processing and payment confirmation.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect users and the platform, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

5. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries outside Nigeria or your country of residence. Where this happens, we rely on the providers and safeguards applicable to the service and take steps required by applicable data-protection law.

6. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain account and payment records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Retention periods can differ by data type. We use access controls and provider security features intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You can update your developer name, bio, portfolio link, profile image and developer-credit preference in your dashboard. You can choose not to publish optional profile details. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of processing of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Contact us through the Help & Support page to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain records where the law allows or requires it.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

DEVPAGES and its service providers may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in, maintain sessions, protect accounts and make the platform work. If we introduce optional analytics or other non-essential technologies, we will update this policy and provide choices where required by law.

9. Children

DEVPAGES is not designed for children who are not legally able to enter into an agreement for the service. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us through the Help page so we can review the matter.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The latest version and effective date will appear on this page. Material changes will be communicated through reasonable means where appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about your personal information, contact DEVPAGES through the Help & Support page.
