Good questions.
Clear answers.

Find answers to common questions about your DEVPAGES account and website address.

How do I get a Pages address? +

Create a DEVPAGES account, choose your preferred name, and follow the checkout steps. DEVPAGES manages the hosting connection for you.

Does a valid name mean it is available? +

No. A name can follow the correct format and still be taken. DEVPAGES checks the real name with Cloudflare during project creation. If it is already in use, you will be asked to choose another name before checkout.

Do I need a Cloudflare account? +

No. DEVPAGES manages the hosting infrastructure. You only need your DEVPAGES account to create and manage your website address.

When do I pay? +

The setup and renewal amounts are shown on the pricing page and at checkout before you pay. Your address is activated after successful payment confirmation, unless your account qualifies for a promotion.
