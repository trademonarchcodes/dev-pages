04 / HELP CENTRE

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Here are a few things to know while DEVPAGES is being prepared for launch.

Can I register a domain now? +

Not yet. Domain discovery is available for testing, but registrar confirmation, checkout and registration are not activated.

Does “unconfirmed” mean available? +

No. It means the current lookup could not confirm availability. A registrar must provide the final answer and price.

Can I connect my Cloudflare account? +

No. Account linking and hosting management are currently offline while those features are rebuilt. Do not submit your Cloudflare password or API token to DEVPAGES.

Can I pay through Paystack? +

Payment processing is not connected yet. Checkout will be enabled only after payment verification and order handling are implemented.
